## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security vulnerabilities seriously and encourage responsible disclosure. If you discover a vulnerability, please follow the process outlined below to report it securely.

### How to Report

1. **Email the Security Team**  
   Report vulnerabilities to our dedicated security team at [security@viktorostergren.io](mailto:security@viktorostergren.io).

2. **Provide Detailed Information**  
   Include the following in your report:
   - A clear description of the vulnerability.
   - Steps to reproduce the issue.
   - The potential impact of the vulnerability.
   - Relevant logs, screenshots, or proof of concept (if applicable).

3. **Encrypt Your Report (Optional)**  
   For sensitive information, you may encrypt your email using our public PGP key, available at [viktorostergren.io/security-key](https://viktorostergren.io/security-key).

**### What to Expect**

- **Acknowledgment**: You will receive a confirmation of your report within **48 hours**.
- **Assessment**: Our security team will investigate and validate the vulnerability within **5 business days**.
- **Updates**: You will receive periodic updates on the status of your report.
- **Resolution**: If the vulnerability is confirmed, we will:
  - Issue a security patch or workaround.
  - Credit you in the release notes, if desired.
  - Provide a timeline for public disclosure.

** Non-Qualifying Issues**

The following are examples of issues not considered security vulnerabilities:
- Denial-of-service (DoS) via large resource consumption unless critical.
- Bugs in dependencies not controlled by the project.

Thank you for helping us improve the security of our project. Together, we can create safer software for everyone!
